Check your CV before you apply.

Use this focused list before your next application reaches a recruiter.

01

First impression

  • Your name, phone number and email are accurate
  • Your professional title or target role is clear
  • The layout is clean and easy to scan
  • Your file has a professional name
02

Content

  • Your career summary matches your direction
  • Experience includes outcomes as well as responsibilities
  • Education, skills and credentials are current
  • Spelling, dates and facts have been checked again
03

Ready to apply

  • Your CV is adapted for the job description
  • A tailored cover letter is ready where needed
  • LinkedIn and portfolio links have been tested
  • PDF and editable copies are stored safely
