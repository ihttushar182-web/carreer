Policies Clear terms, careful handling

Working together with clarity.

These policies explain how Career Minute manages service requests, personal information, delivery and revisions. Last updated: September 17, 2026.

01 Privacy policy

Your information is treated with care.

Career Minute collects only the information needed to understand, quote, produce and deliver a requested service. This may include your name, contact information, career history, CV, portfolio material and payment verification details.

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the people and tools directly involved in delivering your requested service, where necessary. You may request correction or deletion of non-essential stored information by contacting Career Minute, subject to legal, payment and delivery record requirements.

02 Terms & conditions

Clear scope makes better work.

Each service begins after Career Minute confirms the project scope, quote, expected delivery period and any included revisions. Customers are responsible for providing accurate information and for reviewing draft materials before final approval.

Career Minute provides professional presentation, writing and design services. We do not guarantee interviews, admissions, employment, client work or any other third-party outcome.

03 Refund policy

Fair consideration for every request.

Because services are personalized and production work begins after confirmation, refunds are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. If a project has not started, eligibility for a refund may be considered after deducting any confirmed transaction costs. Once approved work, writing, design or development has begun, refunds are generally not available for completed production time.

If Career Minute cannot deliver an agreed scope for a reason under our control, we will work toward a suitable revision, credit or refund solution.

04 Delivery policy

Agreed before the work begins.

Delivery timelines are confirmed after reviewing the requested service, information quality, package scope and current production availability. Final CV and document files are delivered digitally in the agreed formats. Website delivery and handover details are confirmed as part of the agreed website scope.

Delays in receiving required information, approvals or payment verification may change the original delivery timeline.

05 Revision policy

Refinement with a clear process.

Included revisions are confirmed before production begins. A revision is intended to refine the agreed scope, content and design direction; it does not include a new service, a complete change of direction or new material outside the original brief.

Please send consolidated, clear feedback within the agreed review period so the project can continue smoothly.

06 Data & file handling policy

Your documents are not sample material.

CVs, identification details, career documents, project files and private links are treated as confidential service materials. They are accessed only for project delivery and are not published as samples without explicit customer permission.

Customers should avoid submitting passwords, bank account credentials, national identification numbers or other information not needed for the requested service. Files may be retained for a reasonable delivery, support and backup period, then securely removed in line with operational needs.
