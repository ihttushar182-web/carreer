Career documents

Is a CV the same as a resume?+

They are closely related, but requirements can differ by country, industry and application. We’ll guide the structure based on your intended use.

What does ATS-friendly mean?+

ATS-friendly means the content and document structure are designed to be readable by Applicant Tracking Systems while staying clear for human readers.

Can you work from my existing CV?+

Yes. You can attach it when you send your request, and we will use it as a starting point where appropriate.

Can you make a CV if I have no experience?+

Yes. We can build around education, coursework, volunteer work, internships, projects, skills and career goals.

Portfolio websites

Will my portfolio website work on mobile?+

Yes. All portfolio websites are designed responsively for phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can I use my own domain?+

Yes. Domain connection and setup are available within an agreed portfolio scope.

Can I link my portfolio from CV and LinkedIn?+

Absolutely. Your portfolio is designed to be easy to share across applications, LinkedIn and social platforms.

Orders & delivery

What happens after I submit a request?+

Career Minute reviews your information, confirms the scope, quote and delivery process, then provides payment instructions and begins production after confirmation.

How do payments work?+

The first release supports payment verification after your project scope is reviewed. We’ll share the relevant method and steps before work begins.

How many revisions are included?+

Revision availability is confirmed with your selected service scope. We aim to collect the right information early to make the process efficient.
