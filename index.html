07 Need to know

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Still unsure which service is right for you? Send us a note—we’ll point you in the right direction.

Talk to Career Minute →
Is a CV the same as a resume?+

They are closely related, but their format and level of detail can differ by industry and country. We will help you choose the right structure for your target opportunity.

What does ATS-friendly mean?+

Applicant Tracking Systems scan documents before a person sees them. An ATS-friendly document uses clean structure and readable content to support that process without losing visual quality.

Why would I need a portfolio website?+

A portfolio gives recruiters and clients a direct way to see your work, process, credentials and professional point of view. It is especially valuable for creative, technical and freelance roles.

Will my portfolio work on mobile?+

Yes. Every Career Minute portfolio is designed to look and work confidently across phones, tablets and desktops.

What happens after I place an order?+

You will receive a confirmation reference. We review your information, confirm the scope and payment details, then begin the agreed production process.

Can you work with my existing CV?+

Absolutely. Share your current CV when you submit your request. We can use it as a starting point and identify what needs to be strengthened.
